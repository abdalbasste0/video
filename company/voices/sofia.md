# Sofia: the voice of Shortcut HQ

The company's official voice. An original voice, not a clone of anyone. Created in **VoiceStudio** (free, on the owner's computer).

## Voice card
- **Name:** Sofia
- **Language:** English (neutral American, easy for European viewers)
- **Age:** about 25-30
- **Character:** lively, energetic, enthusiastic, smiling while she talks. Speaks fast but clearly, with natural rises in tone at surprises and questions.
- **Speed:** fast for shorts (about 165-175 words per minute). Slow her down 10% for long videos.
- **Avoid:** shouting, ad-voice exaggeration, strong slang, vocal fry at sentence ends.

## Voice Design description (paste as-is into VoiceStudio's voice design box)
> A young American woman in her late twenties with a bright, energetic and upbeat voice. She sounds genuinely excited, like a friend sharing a cool discovery. Fast, punchy delivery with clear articulation, playful rises in pitch on questions, and a natural smile in her tone. Modern social-media creator style, warm, confident, never robotic. Clean studio recording, no background noise.

## Test line (use it to choose between versions)
> Wait... this Black Friday deal might be fake. Here's one prompt that checks it in seconds. Let's go!

## How to create her in VoiceStudio
1. Open VoiceStudio and choose **Voice Design** (designing a voice from a description). If your version doesn't have it, use one of the built-in voices that's closest to the voice card above.
2. Paste the description above, write the test line, and generate 3 or more versions.
3. Pick the best one and save it under the name **Sofia**. Keep a 10-second sample of it in `company/voices/sofia_sample.mp3` so the voice stays identical in every video (you can use the sample for cloning in the future).
4. For each video: paste the text from `company/projects/<project>/vo-script.txt` → choose Sofia → export MP3 → drop the file in `company/inbox/footage/`.

## Rules
- The voice is AI-generated, so turn on the AI label on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.
- Don't use Sofia to imitate a real person, and don't say she's a real person.
