## Opportunity 1 - "I tested [new AI tool] so you don't have to" (30-second verdict)
- **Format:** The verdict comes first ("Saved me 40 minutes / total waste"). Then 3 fast screen-recorded proof beats, a score card and one-line advice on who should use it.
- **Example / evidence:**
  - Faceless guides list "tested/reviewed" and fact/listicle videos as core faceless formats that work. https://kineclip.com/blog/top-ai-tools-faceless-creators-2026/
  - Creators report "I tested X prompts" posts with self-measured lifts. These numbers are unverified. https://www.marcillyaiplaybook.it.com/blog/chatgpt-prompts-content-creators-workflow.html
  - Topical hooks are available now. Aggregators list November 2026 launches, for example a Claude personal-finance tool on Nov 6 and many small agent/voice tools. **Verify on the vendor's own page before scripting.** https://aiindigo.com/changelog?type=news and https://blog.mean.ceo/ai-product-launches-news-november-2026/
- **Why it works:** The viewer gets a payoff (time or money saved, or avoided) in the first second. The "so you don't have to" framing makes it shareable to a friend who was about to try the tool. It also counts as original content because we produced the test ourselves, which matters under Meta's March 2026 originality rules (see #5).
- **How we adapt it:** We record the real test on screen and blur any personal data. A synthetic voiceover gets an "AI-assisted" label. On-screen text: "Tested: <Tool> | Verdict: 7/10". No logos beyond what appears naturally in the screen capture.
- **Best platform:** TikTok first, then Instagram Reels.
- **Hook lines to test:** "I tested <tool> on real work. Here's the honest verdict." / "Don't pay for <tool> until you see this."

## Opportunity 2 - "One prompt that replaces an hour of work" (result first, then the prompt)
- **Format:** A "double hook". Second 0 shows the finished output (a polished email, plan or table), and a text overlay says "This took 1 prompt." The prompt appears on screen long enough to screenshot. The closing CTA is "Send this to the coworker who still does this by hand."
- **Example / evidence:**
  - The "double hook" pattern (result hook, then curiosity hook) was flagged as spreading in mid-2026. This is a vendor source and its +42% claim is unverified. https://www.vo3ai.com/blog/3-step-ugc-tiktok-ad-workflow-with-vo3-ai-inspired-by-twitters-double-hook-trend-2026-06-01
  - Saves-driven prompt posts on Instagram (newsletter case). https://valueflowingai.beehiiv.com/p/3-prompts-that-got-me-847-saves-on-instagram-yesterday
  - According to Buffer's summary, Mosseri named watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** as the top Reels signals. https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/
- **Why it works:** Viewers save and send a prompt they can copy, which feeds the strongest Reels signals. The result-first opening holds viewers past the first 2 seconds.
- **How we adapt it:** Each episode covers one job (freelancer invoice chase, student study plan, marketer brief). This maps to pillars 2 and 3. We put the full prompt in the caption/pinned comment so the video stays short.
- **Best platform:** Instagram Reels, then TikTok.

## Opportunity 3 - Split-screen race: "Manual vs AI" with a timer
- **Format:** Vertical split. The top half shows the task done manually with a running timer, and the bottom half shows the same task with an AI tool. When the AI finishes, a "Done" stamp appears. The voiceover reads one line per stage.
- **Example / evidence:**
  - Before/after transformations are listed as one of the durable "format trends" for 2026. https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-trends and https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-trends/
  - Format-driven trends outlast sound trends, which typically peak within about 7-10 days (SocialPilot tracker, seen in search snippet). https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-trends
- **Why it works:** The contrast reads instantly with the sound off, which matters for Facebook and for EU viewers watching with captions. The timer creates a natural open loop ("how long will AI take?").
- **How we adapt it:** Both halves are our own screen recordings. **Use honest timings only**, with no sped-up AI side unless it is labelled "sped up 2x". We add burned-in captions in English and keep the subtitle files for DE/FR/ES.
- **Best platform:** Facebook Reels (strong with a broader 30+ office-worker audience), then Reels and TikTok.

## Opportunity 4 - Myth vs reality (bold-claim / contrarian hook)
- **Format:** The video opens with a bold claim on screen, for example: "ChatGPT can't do your spreadsheets. (It can do this instead.)" It then gives 2-3 quick "Myth / Reality" cards, each backed by a screen-recorded demo.
- **Example / evidence:**
  - Hook libraries for 2026 list the "bold claim" and "beginner question" hooks as top scroll-stoppers for faceless/slideshow content. https://renderfire.com/resources/digital-marketing/tiktok-hooks
  - Teaching content is called "one of the safest bets" among TikTok trends. https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-trends
- **Why it works:** Viewers comment to agree or disagree, which counts as engagement. It also corrects hype, which builds trust with skeptical EU viewers.
- **How we adapt it:** This is pillar 4. We stay factual, check every claim with a source and avoid naming competitors negatively. No fear-bait about "AI taking your job".
- **Best platform:** TikTok (comment-driven), then Reels.

## Opportunity 5 - Numbered series: "AI news in 60 seconds, Ep. N"
- **Format:** A weekly recurring episode with the same title card, intro line and layout every time ("3 AI updates that matter this week"). Each item gets about 15 seconds and a "should you care?" verdict.
- **Example / evidence:**
  - Series-based content is listed among the main 2026 TikTok trends. https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-trends
  - Some 2026 roundups say 1-3 minute videos now beat 15-second clips on watch time. This is a blog claim, so test it. https://www.kineclip.com/blog/short-form-video-trends-2026
  - Meta (Mar 13, 2026): content a creator produces or significantly transforms through commentary/analysis counts as original, and reposts lose distribution. https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/facebook-makes-it-easier-for-creators-to-report-impersonators/ and https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/meta-updates-policies-to-reward-original-creators-and-limit-reposted-content-152931.html
  - Instagram is reported to down-rank "aggregator" accounts that mostly repost. Thresholds differ between sources and are not confirmed by Instagram. https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/
- **Why it works:** Episodes train viewers to come back and follow ("part 2" / "next week"). Our own commentary turns news into original content, so it is not reposting.
- **How we adapt it:** We show the vendor's own page or our own screen demo and never re-upload other people's clips. Each item carries a source line on screen and the source URL in the caption. The length stays at 60 seconds or less so it fits YouTube Shorts too.
- **Best platform:** Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, also YouTube Shorts.

## Sounds: what is safe for us
- **No trending commercial tracks.** The sound trends I found this month were Halloween-era ("costume loading" freeze-frame) and are fading by mid-November anyway.
- **TikTok:** Use only the **Commercial Music Library (CML)**, which is pre-cleared for business use and is the only library business accounts see in the editor. Note that CML licences are reported to cover **TikTok only**, so a CML track is not cleared for cross-posting. https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-library-explained and https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-copyright
- **Recommendation for one cut posted to 4 platforms:** Use voiceover plus either (a) no music, (b) a royalty-free track whose licence explicitly covers all of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, or (c) a simple original bed we make ourselves. Publish our audio as "original audio" so others can reuse it, which gives us free reach. Add each platform's native library music in-app only if needed, per platform.

## Disclosure
- TikTok requires a label on realistic AI-generated or heavily AI-edited content. It also auto-labels uploads that carry C2PA credentials. A synthetic voiceover or AI-generated visuals should get the AI-generated label or toggle, plus "AI-assisted" in the caption. Plain screen recordings of a tool do not need it. https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-ssa-shares-more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-generated-content?lang=en-ZA and https://billo.app/blog/ai-labeling/
