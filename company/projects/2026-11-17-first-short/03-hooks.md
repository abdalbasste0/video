## Winner: #1 (curiosity gap, result-agnostic)

**Why:** it keeps the title's question, opens a loop that the verdict at 0:31 closes, and stays true whether the demo says REAL, FAKE or NOT SURE, so no re-script after recording.

- VO (12 words, about 3.5 s at Noura's pace): "Is this Black Friday deal real, or fake? One prompt tells you."
- On-screen (from frame 1, big, top third, clear of platform UI): **-40%: REAL or FAKE?**
  - Replace "-40%" with the real discount shown on the demo product page.
- Visual: product page with crossed-out "was" price and discount badge, zoomed. REAL and FAKE stamps flicker over the price (2-3 alternations), no stamp lands. Logos and brand names blurred.
- Do **not** show the chatbot's verdict in the first 3 s. The verdict is the payoff at 0:31.

**Optional upgrade [CONDITIONAL]:** if the recorded demo really returns FAKE DISCOUNT, the editor may A/B test variant #2 as a second cut. Never use #2 if the result is REAL DEAL or NOT SURE.
