## 1. "Is this Black Friday deal fake? Ask AI in 20 seconds"
- **Why now:** Black Friday 2026 is **27 Nov 2026**, 10 days from today ([gptprompts.ai, 2026 guide](https://gptprompts.ai/use-ai-to-find-black-friday-deals-2026)). Search results for AI help with Black Friday shopping are heavy and recurring: Tom's Guide says ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini all have shopping features ([tomsguide.com](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/ai-chatbots-can-do-your-black-friday-shopping-in-minutes-and-chatgpt-perplexity-copilot-and-gemini-all-have-them), date not shown). ChatGPT's shopping research is reported as **available worldwide on free and paid plans** ([tomsguide.com](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-tried-chatgpts-new-shopping-research-feature-it-found-all-of-the-best-black-friday-deals-in-minutes); launch covered by [Axios, 24 Nov 2025](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/24/chatgpt-shopping-research-openai-black-friday)). A 2026 guide covers free AI price tracking ([nexasphere.io, 2026](https://nexasphere.io/blog/ai-price-tracking-save-money-2026)). Kaspersky warns about scams and fake discounts ([kaspersky.com](https://www.kaspersky.com/blog/black-friday-ai-assistance/54798), date not shown).
- **Our angle:** Don't make it "AI shops for you." Make it **"check if the discount is real"**: paste the product link and a screenshot, ask for the price history and three alternatives, and get a verdict in under 60 seconds. Use one copy-paste prompt as the payoff.
- **Demo (free):** free ChatGPT (shopping research) or Google Shopping price insights. Show one product.
- **Risks:** Avoid showing store logos prominently, and blur or crop branded product photos. Don't promise savings. Price-history data varies by country, so say "in the US and many EU countries" and test it on an EU store too. Microsoft Copilot price tracking is **US-only** per [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/best-ai-shopping-assistants/), so don't use it as the main tool.
- **Scores:** demand 9, competition 5, fit 9.

## 3. "Free ChatGPT has a hidden Think button. Use it like this"
- **Why now:** Since August 2026 free users get **unlimited text chats**, **GPT-5.6 Luna** as the default, and a **Think button** for harder questions ([TechCrunch, 6 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-brings-unlimited-chatgpt-text-chats-to-free-users/); [MacRumors, 6 Aug 2026](https://macrumors.com/2026/08/06/chatgpt-free-unlimited-text-chats); [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-rolls-out-a-major-chatgpt-upgrade-even-if-you-dont-pay-for-it)). "What is free on ChatGPT in 2026" is a live search question ([TechRepublic, 13 Aug 2026](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-what-is-free-chatgpt-2026/); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2234173/chatgpt-free-features-2026)).
- **Our angle:** Myth vs reality, as a side-by-side. Give the same tricky question (for example a scheduling or math word problem) to normal mode and Think mode, and show the difference. The lesson: "you don't need to pay for this."
- **Demo (free):** free ChatGPT.
- **Risk:** The news hook is three months old, so this is evergreen rather than breaking news. Check that the button is still labelled "Think" in the UI today. OpenAI moves fast (it shipped GPT Image 2.6 on 4 Nov and ChatGPT Wallet on 13 Nov 2026 per [OpenAI release notes](https://openai.com/products/release-notes/)).
- **Scores:** demand 8, competition 6, fit 8.

## 4. "One prompt that rewrites your CV for any job ad"
- **Why now:** A TikTok storytime about using ChatGPT + Gmail to automate job applications got about **190K views on 14 Oct 2026**, tracked by [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/agent/ai-tools-apps). This is the only dated 2026 outlier signal I found. Job hunting with AI is an evergreen search topic, and late-year job searches add a seasonal lift.
- **Our angle:** Make it safe and honest. Don't mass-apply with a bot. Use one prompt that matches your CV to one job ad, lists missing keywords and rewrites three bullet points without inventing experience.
- **Demo (free):** free ChatGPT or Gemini, with a fake sample CV (never a real person's data).
- **Risks:** This is a crowded topic (competition 4). We win on the "don't lie on your CV" angle and a clean, copyable prompt.
- **Scores:** demand 7, competition 4, fit 9.

It includes a study-notebook feature with diagnostic quizzes. A UK version exists on [blog.google](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-europe/united-kingdom/bringing-the-best-of-ai-to-university-students-free-of-charge/).
- **Our angle:** "Deadline + one feature demo." Spend 10 seconds on how to claim it, then 40 seconds on the quiz/study-plan feature.
- **Risks:** Signing up needs SheerID verification **and a payment method**, and it renews at $19.99/month (AI Pro) or $4.99/month (AI Plus) after the free year. We must say this clearly so it doesn't read as misleading. Eligibility differs by country, which complicates the EU message. The owner can't demo the student signup unless he is a student, so we would demo only the free Gemini features.
