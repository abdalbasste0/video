## 2. Ready replies (6)

Rule for all: reply within the first 1-2 hours after posting if possible. Like the comment first. Keep each reply under 40 words. Never argue twice with the same person.

**R1. "Which chatbot did you use?" / "Does it work in ChatGPT?"**
> Any free chatbot that can search the web works. That's why the prompt says "search the web". In ChatGPT, web search is on the free plan too. Just make sure the answer shows links, then click them. Which one do you use?

(Fact: OpenAI Help Center says web search is available on ChatGPT Free, and people who aren't signed in can use it too, within usage limits. Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search . Before posting, fill in the tool actually used in the demo recording if the owner wants to name it. Don't name a second tool unless we verify it.)

**R2. "Is it free?"**
> Yes, the prompt is free and you don't need a paid plan. Use the free version of any chatbot that can search the web. Free plans have daily limits, so check your deals before Friday, not at checkout 😉

**R3. "Doesn't work in my country" / "It said NOT SURE"**
> Thanks for testing! Price history is easier to find in some countries and stores than others. Try the exact model number instead of the link, and add your country. If it still says NOT SURE, that's the prompt working: no proof = no rush. Which country are you in?

(Collect these countries in a list for Sara/Fahad. See follow-up idea #3.)

**R4. Skeptic: "AI makes things up, this is useless" / "Just use a price tracker"**
> Fair point, AI can be wrong. That's why the prompt asks for a link for every price and says NOT SURE when it can't find data. Treat it as a quick first check, then click the sources. A price tracker is great too if your store has one.

(Do not defend the AI as always right. Do not attack price-tracker tools or name them.)

**R5. "Send the prompt" / "I can't read it" / "Link?"**
> It's in the pinned comment and the caption, ready to copy 👆 Paste it, then fill in the link, the sale price, the "was" price and your country.

(No DMs, no external links, no "comment PROMPT and I'll DM you" automation. Keep it public so others see it.)

**R6. "Is that legal? Can stores do this?" (EU vs US)**
> In the EU, the "was" price must be the lowest price from the last 30 days before the discount. In the US there's no fixed 30-day number, but the FTC says a "was" price must be a real former price. Not legal advice!

(Sources: EU Directive 2019/2161 / 98/6/EC Art. 6a and CJEU C-330/23, US 16 CFR 233.1. Both already cited in `02-script-short.md`. Don't add claims about specific countries' longer periods.)

## 3. Spam, hate and risk handling rule

| Comment type | Action |
|---|---|
| Spam, scam links, "DM me for crypto", fake giveaways, bots posting the same text | **Delete + block/hide.** No reply. Turn on each platform's link/keyword filter (owner sets this in the app). |
| Hate, slurs, harassment, threats (any target) | **Hide/delete + report + block.** No reply. If a threat feels real, flag it to the owner the same day. |
| A viewer names and accuses a specific store ("X store scams people") | **Don't agree or repeat the name.** Reply: "Thanks for sharing. We don't call out specific stores, but always check the sources before you buy." Hide only if it becomes abusive. |
| Rude but on-topic criticism | **Reply once** with the facts (R4 style), then stop. Never mock the commenter. |
| Viewer posts a personal order number, address, email or payment info | **Hide the comment** (protect them) and reply: "Hidden your comment so your personal info stays private." |
| Requests for financial or legal advice | Reply with the general point + "not financial/legal advice". No specific buy/don't-buy decision for someone's money. |

When unsure: hide (it can be unhidden), don't delete, and ask the owner.
