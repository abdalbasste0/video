## Before you press record (2 minutes)

- [ ] Quiet room: close windows, fan/AC off, phone on silent. Soft room (curtains, sofa, wardrobe) is better than a bare room.
- [ ] Phone mic **at least 15 cm** from your mouth, slightly to the side (stops "p" pops). Same distance for every take.
- [ ] Voice memo app or any recorder, highest quality (WAV / lossless if offered).
- [ ] Glass of water. Read the whole script once out loud before the first take.
- [ ] Record **3 takes of the hook** (block 1), then 1-2 takes of the rest. Leave 2 s of silence at the start of each file.
- [ ] If you trip on a word: pause, say the whole line again. Khaled cuts the bad one.
- [ ] Write the time of the chatbot answer here: ____ s (needed for the title, see `03-hooks.md`).

How to read the marks:
- One line = one breath group. Read at a calm, friendly pace.
- **bold** = stress this word a little.
- [pause] = short pause, about half a second.
- [beat] = longer pause, about 1 second (a visual change happens there).

Paste everything between the two `---` lines into your teleprompter app.
