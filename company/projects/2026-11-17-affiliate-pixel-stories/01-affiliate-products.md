## Scoring (1-10 each, total out of 40)
- **Commission value**: the money per conversion, plus whether it recurs and for how long.
- **Conversion ease**: a free plan or trial, a low price and broad appeal.
- **Story strength**: how dramatic the history is (pivots, near-death moments, big numbers) and how well it is documented.
- **Fit**: how well it matches the AI tools & productivity niche and a US/EU creator audience.

## Product cards

### 1. ElevenLabs: AI voice, dubbing and audio generation
- **Program**: https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates · terms https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates-terms · guide https://elevenlabs.io/affiliate-partner-guide (checked 2026-11-17 via an official-domain search)
- **Commission**: 22% on Starter/Creator/Pro/Scale for the referred subscriber's first 12 months. 11% on Business. Nothing on Enterprise. ElevenLabs can change rates "at its discretion".
- **Cookie**: not stated in the terms. The PartnerStack guide counts sign-ups within 90 days of a click. Treat 90 days as unconfirmed.
- **Payout**: via PartnerStack, **$5 minimum**. Commissions unlock 90 days after the referral's first payment (chargeback window).
- **Who can join**: open application on PartnerStack. PartnerStack pays globally, so US and EU are fine. Approval is required.
- **Free plan**: yes, a free tier with monthly credits.
- **Story hooks**:
  1. Two childhood friends from Poland (Mati Staniszewski, Piotr Dąbkowski) hated Polish "lektor" dubbing: one flat male voice reading every character in a foreign film. That frustration became the company.
  2. Founded in 2022. By **February 2026** it raised a **$500M Series D at an $11B valuation**, led by Sequoia.
  3. In 2026 the Polish state took a stake in ElevenLabs to boost Poland's tech hub. A rare "home country buys back in" moment.
- **Risks**: the terms ban cookie stuffing, self-referral and false coupon or cash-back promises. Voice cloning is a sensitive topic, so show only consented or stock voices. Our own house voice tool is VoiceStudio, so we must not imply our videos are voiced with ElevenLabs.
- **Sources**: https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates-terms · https://elevenlabs.io/affiliate-partner-guide · https://fintool.com/news/elevenlabs-11b-valuation-series-d · https://www.markhub24.com/post/two-polish-boys-hated-how-their-movies-sounded-so-they-built-a-11-billion-voice-ai-company-the-s · https://cryptobriefing.com/poland-elevenlabs-stake-ai-hub/

### 4. Descript: edit video and podcasts by editing the text transcript
- **Program**: https://www.descript.com/affiliate · terms https://www.descript.com/affiliate-terms (checked 2026-11-17 via an official-domain search)
- **Commission**: **flat $25 one-time** per new qualifying subscription. No renewals. Annual cap $100,000. Paid via PartnerStack. (The old 15% recurring is gone. Older blog lists are stale.)
- **Cookie**: 30 days. **Payout minimum**: none reported (3rd-party).

- **Free plan**: yes.
- **Story hooks**:

  3. Raised from **a16z** and later the **OpenAI Startup Fund** (2022). From the "failed CEO" to a tool used by millions of creators.
- **Risks**: the terms **ban paid search bidding and coupon-site promotion**. The low one-time payout means it needs volume. Descript also runs a separate user-referral program, and you cannot mix the two links.
