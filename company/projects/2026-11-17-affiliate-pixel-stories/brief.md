## Owner request (2026-11-17)
"Produce videos like this one. I need 10 videos, and research 10 products for affiliate marketing."

## Reference video (company/inbox/footage/reference-nokia-pixel-3min.mp4, not committed)
- 3:06, 1920x1080, 24 fps, narrated, no face.
- Style: pixel-art animated **company story** (rise and fall of Nokia): side-view rooms (CEO suite, engineering, boardroom, factory, deal room), tiny pixel characters at desks, a big date card on the wall (2007, 1992, SEP 2010...), board charts (bar chart, falling line), big money numbers ("7.2 BILLION", "-7.6 BILLION"), a burning-platform scene at night.
- We make **original** stories in this *genre* (pixel-art business storytelling). We do not copy Nokia's scenes, script or look one-to-one.

## Format decision (Yasmin)
- 10 videos, ~2:30-3:00 each, 16:9 for YouTube + Facebook, plus a 9:16 60s cut of each for Shorts / TikTok / Reels.
- Each video = the true story of the company behind one affiliate product (founding → struggle → breakthrough → numbers), ending with "how you can use it today" + affiliate link in description/pinned comment.
- Products: AI tools & productivity SaaS with affiliate programs open to US/EU creators (fits our niche, recurring commissions).
- Voice: Sofia (VoiceStudio, owner generates). Videos are rendered here with on-screen text + burned captions and a VO slot; final mix after owner drops the Sofia MP3.
- Legal: affiliate disclosure on screen + in description (FTC in the US, EU consumer law). AI label (AI voice). No company logos as artwork, only names in text.
